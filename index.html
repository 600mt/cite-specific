Matteo Bettini is an information designer and visual artist based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Trained in graphic design and typography, he has an enduring obsession with maps and the worlds they construct. Through visual and spatial research, he explores the tension between map and territory, questioning how we see, interpret and represent the world.
