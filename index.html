Matteo Bettini is an information designer and visual artist based in the Netherlands, trained in graphic design and typography. His mapping and information design practice explores what lies between the map and the territory: perception, narrative, ambiguity and power in how places are represented. He works between the Netherlands and Italy and is part of a collective based in Milan.

Clients I worked for:

International organizations: United Nations Satellite Center, UNOSAT, Human Rights Watch

Design studios: Studio Folder(IT), DotDotDot (IT), Non-Linear (CH)

Universities: Design Academy Eindhoven (NL), University of Amsterdam (NL), Politecnico di Milano (IT), Politecnico di Torino (IT), Università di Pisa (IT), Sheffield University (UK)
